# Regulatory Baseline (IND Nonclinical)

## Use This File For
- Interpreting what evidence is expected for IND nonclinical sections.
- Checking whether planned summaries address FDA and ICH expectations.
- Building defensible rationale for study selection, species relevance, and safety margins.

## Core FDA Baseline
- 21 CFR 312.23(a): structure and content requirements for IND submissions.
- 21 CFR 312.23(a)(8): pharmacology and toxicology information expected in IND.
- 21 CFR Part 58: GLP expectations for nonclinical laboratory studies.

## Core ICH Baseline
- ICH M3(R2): timing and scope of nonclinical studies to support clinical trials.
- ICH S6(R1): preclinical safety for biotechnology-derived products.
- ICH S7A/S7B: safety pharmacology and QT/hERG considerations.
- ICH S2(R1): genotoxicity testing and interpretation.
- ICH S5(R3): reproductive/developmental toxicity framework.
- ICH S9: nonclinical evaluation for anticancer pharmaceuticals (where applicable).

## Apply Requirements to CTD Sections
- CTD 2.4: emphasize integrated interpretation and risk framing.
- CTD 2.6: emphasize study-level detail, tabulated parameters, and traceability.
- Module 4 references: anchor claims to report sections and study identifiers.

## Evidence Minimums by Domain
- Pharmacology:
  - Mechanism, target engagement, translational relevance.
  - Safety pharmacology domains when applicable (CV/CNS/respiratory).
- PK/TK:
  - Core parameters (`Cmax`, `AUC`, `Tmax`, `t1/2`, clearance/volume when available).
  - Exposure margins and relation to proposed clinical dosing.
- Toxicology:
  - Study design, species/route/duration.
  - NOAEL/LOAEL, target organs, reversibility, and dose relationship.

## Compliance-Oriented Writing Rules
- Distinguish observed data from interpretation.
- Tie each key claim to report evidence or database trace.
- Identify missing studies or unresolved gaps explicitly.
- Avoid legal conclusions; provide evidence-based readiness assessment.
