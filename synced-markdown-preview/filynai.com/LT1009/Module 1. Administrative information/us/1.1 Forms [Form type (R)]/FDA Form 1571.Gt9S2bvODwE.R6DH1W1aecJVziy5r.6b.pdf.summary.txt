Document Summary
=================
This document outlines the requirements for submitting an Investigational New Drug Application (IND) to the FDA, as per Title 21 CFR Part 312. It specifies that no drug may be shipped or clinical investigations commenced until an IND is in effect. The application must include detailed information about the drug, the sponsor, and the intended clinical investigation phases. Additionally, it mandates that submissions be numbered consecutively and provides guidelines for various types of submissions, including amendments and safety reports. The document also notes the expiration date of the form and includes a statement regarding the OMB approval.
