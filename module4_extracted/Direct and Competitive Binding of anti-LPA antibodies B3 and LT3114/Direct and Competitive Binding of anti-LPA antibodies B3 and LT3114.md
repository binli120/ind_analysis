1 SUMMARY
Lpath has developed murine and humanized monoclonal antibodies targeting LPA. This
report covers development of ELISA methods that are both sensitive and specific for Acyl
chain LPA isoforms. The Ki of both the murine and humanized antibodies range from
15.8 nM to 986nM for all of the isoforms tested
2 INTRODUCTION
Lysophosphatidic acid (LPA) is not a single molecular entity but a collection of structural
analogs with a single functional glycerol alcohol phosphate moiety esterified to a variety of
fatty acyl hydrocarbons of varied lengths and degrees of saturation. LPA species with both
saturated (16:0 and 18:0) and unsaturated fatty acids (16:1, 18:1, 18:2, and 20:4) have been
identified in biological fluids with 16:0, 18:1 18:2 and 20:4 LPA being the predominant
species. Lpath has generated murine and humanized monoclonal antibodies to
lysophosphatidic acid and this report describes data showing that these antibodies bind to all
of the relevant isoforms of LPA.

3.1 Test Article and Control

3.2 Study Design
Development of both direct and competition ELISA format assays to demonstrate binding
and specificity of anti-LPA antibodies to LPA ligands.
3.3 Direct Binding ELISA
The assay uses thiolated C12-LPA conjugated to BSA as the capture agent for the direct
format ELISA. Ninety six well plates were coated with 100 µl of 1 µg/ml of C12 LPA-BSA-
SMCC for 1 hour at 37 degrees. The plates were washed 4 times with PBS and blocked with
150 µl of PBS-BSA-Tween for 1 hour at room temperature. 100 µl of B3 or 3114 antibody
was added starting at 4 µg/ml and diluted 2-fold across the plate for the standard curve.
Plates were incubated at room temperature for 1 hour. Next, plates where washed 4 times
with PBS and 100µl of a peroxidase conjugated anti-mouse IgG (H+L) (Jackson
immunoresearch 115-035-062) or anti-human IgG (H+L) (Jackson immunoresearch 709-035-
149) was added at a 1:40K dilution and incubated for 1 hour at room temperature. Finally,
the plates were washed four times with PBS and developed with 100 µl cold TMB substrate
for 12 minutes at room temperature. The reaction was stopped with 100 µl of 1M H2SO4.
Plates were read at 450 nm.
3.4 Competition ELISA
The specificity and binding of the murine (B3) and humanized (LT3114) anti-LPA
monoclonal antibodies were determined by competition ELISA. For LT3114 competition,
goat anti-human IgG (Jackson Immunoresearch, Cat#109-005-098, Lot # 90887) was diluted
to 1 µg/mL in carbonate buffer (100 mM NaHCO3, 33.6 mM Na2CO3, pH 9.5) and
15 uL/well of the coating solution was added to 384 well plates and incubated overnight at
4°C. The following day, the plates were blocked with 60 µL/well PBS + 1% BSA + 0.1%
Tween-20 for 1 hour at room temperature. The human 3114 antibody was diluted to
50 ng/mL in PBS +0.1 % Tween-20 and 15 µL/well were added and incubated for 1 hour at
room temperature. Then, the plates were washed 4X with PBS and incubated for 2 hour at
room temperature with 15 µL/well LPA-biotin (20 nM) /unlabeled (2000 to 0.1 nM) lipid
mixture. Next, plates were washed 4X with PBS and 15 µL/well SA-HPX 1/30,000 was
added and incubated for 15 min at room temperature. Again the plates were washed 4X with
PBS and 15 µL/well cold TMB substrate was added. After 5 minutes, the reaction was
stopped with 1M H SO . The optical density (OD) was measured at 450 nm using a Thermo

4 RESULTS
Binding of Anti-LPA antibodies to LPA by Direct ELISA
The murine mAb used for proof-of-concept studies in animal models of neuropathic pain
(designated B3) and the humanized variant (LT3114) shows similar binding characteristics
by direct ELISA. Binding curves were comparable and all fit well to the 4-parameter model
with a Hill slope of ~1 and narrow 95% confidence limits for comparable EC50 (33.5 ng/ml
for B3 and 24.4 ng/ml for LT3114) (Figure 1, A and B).
Binding Affinities of Anti-LPA antibodies to LPA Isoforms by Competition ELISA
The competition binding ELISA was used to compare the affinity of the murine (B3) and
humanized (LT3114) antibody for different isoforms of LPA. Figure 2 shows the competition
binding curves for 16:0, 18:0, 18:1, 18:2 and 20:4 LPA isoforms. The humanized antibody,
LT3114, and the murine antibody, B3, from which the LT3114 CDRs were obtained, have
essentially identical binding characteristics for native lipid, with a Ki of 16 nM for 18:2 LPA,
360 nM for 18:1 LPA (Table 1).
